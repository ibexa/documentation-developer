# Shipping management

Shipping management module handles shipping methods.

Editions: Commerce

Shipping processes are present in almost every e-commerce business. Ibexa DXP comes with a number of storefront and backend features to help users manage shipping.

No two businesses are identical, and Ibexa ensures the flexibility that you need to configure shipping methods in a way that is optimal for your business and store customers.

If the permissions assigned to your [user role](../../../permission_management/permissions_and_users/index.md) allow, the **Shipping management** module enables you to define and manage shipping methods of different types on the **Shipping methods** screen.

![Shipping methods list](https://doc.ibexa.co/projects/userguide/en/5.0/commerce/img/shipping_methods_list.png "Shipping methods list")

The **Shipping management** module interacts with other packages of the system, so that shipment processing is cancelled automatically when the customer cancels their order.

- [Work with shipping methods](../work_with_shipping_methods/index.md): Define new shipping methods or modify existing ones.
- [Work with shipments](../work_with_shipments/index.md): Work with shipments and view shipment details.
