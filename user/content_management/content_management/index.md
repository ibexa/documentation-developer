# Content management

Content management enables you to create, edit, and manage content items and content types. It also includes content translation and publication scheduling.

- [Content items](../content_items/index.md): Content items are containers that Ibexa DXP uses to store content data.
- [Create and edit content items](../create_edit_content_items/index.md): Create content for your website with different fields, rich text, tags, and then publish it.
- [Create and edit pages](../create_edit_pages/index.md): Create multi-zone Pages with dynamic blocks. Use them as landing pages.
- [Block reference](../block_reference/index.md): Pick from a list of dynamic page blocks to create landing pages.
- [Preview content items](../preview_content_items/index.md): Preview content items of various types in many places of the back office.
- [Work with forms](../work_with_forms/index.md): Use surveys, questionnaires, sign-up forms and other interactive forms to enrich your website.
- [Translate content](../translate_content/index.md): Create multiple language versions of content items and products.
