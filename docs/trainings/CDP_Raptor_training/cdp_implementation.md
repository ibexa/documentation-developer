# Product Overview – Raptor Personalization portfolio

At Raptor, personalization is at the core of everything we do.
Our product portfolio is designed to deliver relevant, data-driven experiences across every customer touchpoint:

- **Recommendations** - allow you to deliver personalized product suggestions across channels such as web and email, using real-time behavioral data to drive engagement and conversions.

- **Triggers** - with triggers you can activate real-time trigger-based actions when users engage with specific content, products, or services—perfect for follow-ups like cart abandonment, price drop alerts, or back-in-stock notifications.

-**Site Search** - this tool is powered by the open-source Typesense framework, our intelligent site search delivers fast, relevant results tailored to each user’s intent, improving product discovery and user satisfaction.

-**Merchandising center** - supports the retail media strategy by allowing brands to boost specific products or campaigns—helping highlight high-margin items or strategic brands directly within the customer journey.

-**Customer Data Platform (CDP)** - centralizes customer data from multiple sources to build unified profiles, drive advanced segmentation, and activate personalized experiences across owned and paid channels.

# Customer Data Platform (CDP)

The idea behind CDP is to unify customer data from multiple sources and activate it across owned and paid channels.
We can ingest data from systems like CRM, loyalty programs, and transactional records.
At the core, our technology acts like a "keychain," stitching data together around each individual using identifiers such as email or phone number, ensuring profiles are only matched when there's a 100% verified match for accuracy.

Once unified, these customer profiles can be activated directly in platforms like CMS, email systems, and paid channels such as Google and LinkedIn, enabling highly targeted marketing.
The CDP is agnostic in both data ingestion and activation, and it uses AI to enrich profiles with insights and predictions, enhancing the precision and personalization of customer engagement.

## CDP - key benefits

CDP brings numerous benefits that support more efficient and effective use of customer data at scale.

Key benefits of using CDP are:

- Ingesting customer data from different sources such as CRM systems, loyalty programs, and transactions
- Unifying customer profiles through verified matches using identifiers like email or phone number
- Enriching profiles with AI-driven predictions and advanced customer insights
- Activating real-time engagement across owned and paid channels, including email, CMS, Google, and LinkedIn
- Ensuring data accuracy and compliance by performing matches only when full certainty is achieved
- Maintaining platform-agnostic architecture for seamless integration with existing technology stacks

# Control Panel

## Control Panel - what is it

Control Panel is a centralized interface for managing Raptor's personalization and CDP features, designed to streamline workflows for marketing, data, and IT teams.
It provides users with a streamlined interface to access and manage all personalization and CDP tools in one place.
The built-in news dashboard keeps users updated on new features, product updates, and relevant releases—ensuring teams stay informed and in control of their personalization efforts.

Users navigate through a collapsible sidebar that groups key features such as Data Manager, Operational Insights, Trust Center, and User Management.

Control Panel consists of:

- a collapsible sidebar with grouped dropdowns for intuitive navigation
- a dashboard showcasing the latest news and product updates
- tools section, including:
    - **Data Manager** – ingest, map, and cleanse data for CDP or recommendation engines
    - **Operational Insights** – monitor service performance, detect, and resolve errors in real time
    - **Trust Center** – manage GDPR compliance and security settings
    - **User Management** – control access permissions and roles for internal and external users
