# Ibexa DXP editions

Learn more about various Ibexa DXP editions' features to help yourself choose the right one for your project.

Three Ibexa DXP product editions are available to help you accelerate your digital transformation at the speed and cost that work best for you.

- [Ibexa Headless edition product guide](../ibexa_headless/index.md): Get to know Ibexa Headless - an edition that focuses on content management.
- [Ibexa Experience edition product guide](../ibexa_experience/index.md): Learn about all the main attributes, features, and benefits of the customer-focused Ibexa Experience edition.
- [Ibexa Commerce edition product guide](../ibexa_commerce/index.md): Explore all of the key features, functionalities, and advantages of Ibexa Commerce, the most powerful edition that Ibexa DXP has to offer.
