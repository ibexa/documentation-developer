# Product guides

Discover various Ibexa DXP features.

Ibexa DXP product editions come with a variety of features. Discover the primary ones with the help of product guides. Condensed content allows you to quickly learn about their availability, capabilities, and benefits.

- [User management product guide](../../users/user_management_guide/index.md): Find out what's user management and check what functions Ibexa DXP offers in this area to effectively manage the digital ecosystem.
- [Content management product guide](../../content_management/content_management_guide/index.md): Read the content management product guide and learn how to create, modify, and display information to the target audience.
- [Discounts product guide](../../discounts/discounts_guide/index.md): Discount enable reducing prices on products or product categories based on a detailed logic resolution.
- [Online Editor product guide](../../content_management/rich_text/online_editor_guide/index.md): Learn how to use the Online Editor, a tool that allows you to edit RichText Fields in any content item in Ibexa DXP.
- [Page Builder product guide](../../content_management/pages/page_builder_guide/index.md): Read about the Page Builder - a powerful tool for creating and modifying pages in Ibexa DXP.
- [Form Builder product guide](../../content_management/forms/form_builder_guide/index.md): See the Form Builder product guide and learn how to create various forms to increase the functionality of your website.
- [Collaborative editing product guide](../../content_management/collaborative_editing/collaborative_editing_guide/index.md): The Collaborative editing product guide provides a full description of the features and benefits that this module brings to the clients.
- [Customer Portal](../../customer_management/customer_portal/index.md): Customer Portal allows your business clients to create and manage their company accounts.
- [Product catalog guide](../../product_catalog/product_catalog_guide/index.md): The product catalog guide provides a full description of the features and capabilities for managing products, their specifications, variants, pricing, and organization.
- [Quable product guide](../../product_catalog/quable/quable_guide/index.md): The Quable product guide describes how you can use the product data from Quable in Ibexa DXP to create marketing campaigns built around your products.
- [Shopping list feature guide](../../commerce/shopping_list/shopping_list_guide/index.md): A shopping list allows users to save potential purchases, recurring product sets, and other items for future use in the cart.
- [Ibexa Cloud product guide](../../ibexa_cloud/ibexa_cloud_guide/index.md): Learn how to host your application and improve your business processes by using Ibexa Cloud hosting platform.
- [Ibexa Customer Data Platform (CDP) product guide](../../cdp/cdp_guide/index.md): The Ibexa CDP product guide describes all the possibilities that the Customer Data Platform offers to help you build great customer experiences.
- [Raptor integration guide](../../recommendations/raptor_integration/raptor_connector_guide/index.md): Discover Raptor integration - an add-on focused on recommendations and tracking customer behaviors.
- [AI Actions product guide](../../ai/ai_actions/ai_actions_guide/index.md): AI Actions help editors by automating repetitive tasks.
- [MCP Servers product guide](../../ai/mcp/mcp_guide/index.md): MCP servers expose tools, specialized prompts, and resources to AI agents.
