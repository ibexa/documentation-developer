# Product Catalog

Ibexa DXP provides product catalog capabilities for managing products, product types, variants, attributes, pricing, and catalogs.

The Product Catalog provides comprehensive capabilities for managing products offered in your digital commerce experience, including their specifications, pricing, and organization.

Ibexa DXP offers robust product catalog infrastructure that can be used standalone.

You can also use [Quable Product Information Management (PIM)](../quable/quable/index.md) add-on that fully integrates into the Ibexa ecosystem, or the [Remote PIM](../add_remote_pim_support/index.md) to add integration with any external PIM system.

- [Product catalog guide](../product_catalog_guide/index.md): The product catalog guide provides a full description of the features and capabilities for managing products, their specifications, variants, pricing, and organization.
- [Quable PIM Integration](../quable/quable/index.md): Quable PIM integration with Ibexa DXP
- [Products](../products/index.md): Products are characterized by attributes describing their characteristics. You can create product variants and add assets to each product and variant.
- [Catalogs](../catalogs/index.md): Catalogs enable filtering our a selection of products from the PIM.
- [Product catalog configuration](../product_catalog_configuration/index.md): Configure product catalog settings per repository, with different catalog engines and VAT configurations.
- [Prices](../prices/index.md): The price engine calculates product prices taking into account customer groups, currencies and taxes.
