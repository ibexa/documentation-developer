# Commerce

The commerce component of Ibexa DXP covers various steps of making a transaction from listing available products, through adding products to a cart, to checkout and confirmation.

Editions: Commerce

The commerce component of Ibexa DXP covers various areas of managing an e-commerce presence: from configuring payment and shipping methods, through processing a transaction (listing available products, adding products to a cart, processing checkout, and sending confirmation), all the way to order management.

- [Cart](../cart/cart/index.md): The cart component covers adding items to the shopping cart, and previewing or modifying the cart information.
- [Shopping list](../shopping_list/shopping_list/index.md): Shopping list allows users to save potential purchases, recurring product sets, and other items for future use in the cart.
- [Checkout](../checkout/checkout/index.md): The checkout component covers providing shipping and billing addresses, and selecting payment and shipping methods.
- [Order management](../order_management/order_management/index.md): The order management component covers creating orders and managing their lifecycle.
- [Payment](../payment/payment/index.md): The payment component covers defining and managing payment methods, together with managing payments and their lifecycle.
- [Shipping](../shipping_management/shipping_management/index.md): The shipping component covers defining and managing shipping methods, together with managing shipments and their lifecycle.
- [Storefront](../storefront/storefront/index.md): Storefront covers actions related to the purchase process.
- [Transactional emails](../transactional_emails/transactional_emails/index.md): With transactional emails you can notify end users about changes in the status of user registration, password recovery, orders, payments, shipments, and more.
